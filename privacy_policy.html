Privacy Policy for Doodle Craft

Last Updated: September 15, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Doodle Craft. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

No Data Collection

Doodle Craft is designed to be a completely offline application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or data from you or your device. All drawings and data you create within the app are stored exclusively on your own device.

Permissions

Doodle Craft may request the following permissions:

  • Storage (Optional): We request storage access only when you explicitly choose to save or export a drawing to your device's public gallery. This permission is not required for the core functionality of the app, and we do not access any other files on your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: doodle.craft.support@example.com
